# Transportation Analytics & Mobility Lab (TAM Lab)

Website of the Transportation Analytics & Mobility Lab at the Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility, KAIST, led by Dr. Reuben Tamakloe.

**Visit the site:** https://drtamakloe.github.io/

We combine statistics, machine learning, and transportation engineering across four research lines: traffic safety and injury severity; data analytics, machine learning, and pattern discovery; urban mobility systems and public transport; and emerging mobility technologies, automation, and electric vehicles.

Prospective students: see https://drtamakloe.github.io/#join

Site content is edited in `assets/tamlab/lab-data.js`. See `HOW-TO-UPDATE.md`.
